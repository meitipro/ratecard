## FAQ

**Is it a vote?** No. Validators each compare the claim with the same five examples and answer one tier. A size stands only when they agree on the label.

**Can the team veto a claim?** Yes, once, inside the review window, for any reason. After the window the claim is sized and paid.

**What if I disagree with my size?** You can appeal once for the card's appeal fee. The claim is read again with the ladder shown largest first, and the second reading replaces the first.

**Do my hours count?** No. The judge is told to ignore hours and effort the claimant says it took, and any claim about price.

**Who sets the price?** The team, when the card opens. The model only chooses a tier.

**Why can anyone call `size`?** So a size does not wait for the contributor to return, and it is a function of what is already on chain. The caller is recorded.

**Which network?** GenLayer Studio Next, chain 61997. Test GEN is free from the site's faucet.
