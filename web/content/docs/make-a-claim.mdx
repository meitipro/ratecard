---
title: Make a claim
description: Describe the work you did, wait out the review window, read the size, and withdraw the price.
---

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Read the card.** The card page shows the five example jobs and the price of each, the cap, the review window and the appeal fee. Your claim will be compared with exactly these.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Describe the work.** Press **Make a claim** and write what you did in your own words, at most 600 characters. Say what the work was, not how hard it was: size is scope and effort like the examples, and the hours you report are ignored by rule. **Test against the ladder** shows where one model would place it, as a preview that is never binding.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Wait out the window.** The claim enters the card's review window. The owner can veto it once, inside the window, for any reason. A claim reserves nothing yet.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Read the size.** After the window anyone can press **Size this claim**. The page shows **Validators are sizing**, then the ladder with a marker on the example your claim landed on, and the price credited to your balance.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Withdraw.** **Withdraw** on [My claims](/me) pays your balance, a top-level transfer of what is yours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If you disagree

You can appeal once. It costs the card's appeal fee and the claim is read again with the ladder shown largest first. The second reading replaces the first. If the result changed, the fee comes back to you; if not, it goes to the pool. See [Review windows and appeals](/docs/concepts/windows-and-appeals).

## If the claim is UNSIZED

The text was not a description of work, or gave too little to compare. Nothing is paid and the claim is closed. You may write a new claim.
